AAYU · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy describes how AAYU handles information when you use the AAYU mobile application. AAYU is a personal wellness app. Please review this policy before connecting an account or enabling a backup.

Information AAYU handles

  • Health Connect records: if you grant permission, AAYU reads supported records available in Health Connect, which may include activity, sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, skin temperature and other supported health metrics. The exact records depend on your device, connected apps and permissions.
  • Information you enter: manual vitals and logs, journal answers, symptoms, mood entries, workout details, goals and other information you choose to save.
  • App preferences: settings such as appearance, selected journal questions, goals, sleep-planner choices and other preferences.
  • Google account authorization: if you connect Google Drive, Google provides authorization needed to store and retrieve AAYU’s backup. AAYU does not need access to your ordinary Drive files for this backup.

How information is used

AAYU uses health records and information you enter to show wellness scores, trends, summaries and app features. The app may store this information in its local app storage so it can display your history and settings.

Health Connect

Health Connect is an Android service. Your permissions determine which records AAYU can read. AAYU’s use of Health Connect data is limited to providing and improving the features you use. AAYU does not sell Health Connect data or use it for advertising.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you connect Google Drive and enable backup, AAYU can copy your saved AAYU history—including supported Health Connect history, manual entries, journal entries, workouts and preferences—to the private app-specific data area for AAYU in the Google account you choose. The backup is not encrypted by AAYU before upload. Google stores it under the Google account you authorized and its services are subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy. Anyone with access to that Google account may be able to access its app data through Google or the account holder’s tools.

You can choose the backup frequency in AAYU, run a backup manually, restore a backup, or disconnect Google Drive in the app. Automatic backups depend on Android background execution and a working Google authorization. Disconnecting Drive stops future access but does not necessarily erase an existing backup. Remove AAYU’s access or app data from your Google Account settings if you want to revoke access or remove stored app data.

Sharing and service providers

AAYU does not sell your personal or health information. When you use Google Drive backup, the selected Google account receives the backup so the feature can work. Optional features that you choose to connect—such as an AI provider configured by you—may send the specific information you submit to that provider under its own terms. Review the provider’s policy before using such a feature.

AAYU may send limited diagnostic and performance information to Sentry to identify app failures. This can include error codes, technical device/app details and sampled performance traces; it is designed not to include health values, journal answers, names, email addresses or photos. Diagnostic reporting is enabled by default and can be turned off in the app’s privacy settings. Crash reports may include technical error messages and stack details.

Retention and deletion

Your app data remains in AAYU’s local storage until you delete it, clear or uninstall the app, or otherwise remove it. A Drive backup remains in Google Drive until it is deleted there or through controls Google provides. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete a Drive backup, and deleting a Drive backup does not delete local app data.

Security

AAYU uses Android app storage and Google’s app-specific Drive storage for the functions described above. Drive backups are not encrypted by AAYU before upload. Keep your device and Google account secure. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

AAYU is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when AAYU’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
